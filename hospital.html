Roots & Heritage

25 years of clinical dedication

Founded in 2001 as a 60-bed community surgical clinic, NovaMed grew from a conviction that complex medical cases demand unified collaboration between cardiologists, neurologists, surgeons, and primary physicians.

Over two decades, we expanded into a 320-bed JCI-accredited Multi-Specialty Hospital featuring hybrid operating theaters, Level-1 emergency trauma bays, and a fully integrated digital Patient Portal.

Today, more than 140 senior consultants and 650 specialized nurses serve over 50,000 families each year with unwavering integrity.
